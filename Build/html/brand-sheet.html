Photo rules

Natural skin, real face — no heavy beautify filters. Indigo or ivory backgrounds. Eyes sharp, calm expression. Always inside an arch or circle frame with the gold double line.

Words we use

Say

“Support for migraine & chronic pain”
“Sugar (diabetes): stress & cravings support”
“Depression & low mood support”
“May help”, “can support”, “alongside your doctor’s care”
“Certified Therapist · IPHM (UK) accredited training”
“Inner child healing”, “release”, “remember your strength”

Avoid

“Cure diabetes / sugar healing” — diabetes is on the Drugs & Magic Remedies Act schedule
“Guaranteed”, “100% result”, “permanent relief”
“Stop your medicines” or anything implying it
“Dr.” — not a medical title for this practice
Before/after health claims or testimonials as proof
Every piece that names a health condition carries the “alongside your doctor” line.
